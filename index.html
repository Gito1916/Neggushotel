Beyond the Stay

While Neggus Hotel offers a retreat from the world, the rich culture and nature of Calabar await just beyond our doors.

01 — Discover

Calabar — The People's Paradise

Known as the cleanest city in Nigeria, Calabar is a vibrant coastal city steeped in history and natural beauty. From the iconic Millennium Park to the historic Duke Town Church, every corner tells a story of culture and heritage.

Home to the famous Calabar Carnival — Africa's biggest street party — this city comes alive with colour, music, and an infectious energy that captivates every visitor.

02 — Experience

Tinapa Resort & Waterpark

Tinapa is one of Nigeria's largest leisure and entertainment destinations. Built along the Calabar River, it features thrilling water slides, wave pools, and a sprawling waterpark that's perfect for families and adventure seekers alike.

Beyond the rides, Tinapa offers a cinema, shopping mall, and waterfront restaurants — making it an unforgettable day trip just minutes from the hotel.

Plan Your Visit arrow_forward
03 — Explore

Obudu Mountain Resort

Perched at over 1,500 metres above sea level, the Obudu Mountain Resort (formerly Obudu Cattle Ranch) offers a breathtaking escape into the clouds. Cool mountain air, lush green hills, and stunning panoramic views make it one of Nigeria's most spectacular destinations.

Enjoy Africa's longest cable car ride, swim in natural pools, or hike through pristine forests — a day trip from Calabar that feels like another world entirely.
